title: Laptop refresh cycle
department: IT
classification: internal
groups: all-employees
updated: 2026-01-20
---
Company laptops are replaced every three years. Engineering, data science and design staff are on a 30-month cycle because their workloads are heavier.

You will receive an email from IT when your device is eligible. To request a replacement earlier because of a hardware fault, open the ServiceDesk catalog item HW-REFRESH and describe the problem. Early replacements need manager approval.

Return your old device within 14 days of receiving the new one. IT wipes and recycles returned devices; do not wipe it yourself.
