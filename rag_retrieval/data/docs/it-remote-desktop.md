Hardware lab machines in the Austin and Dublin offices can be reached with remote desktop. Book a machine in the lab calendar first, then connect through the Guacamole web portal.

Sessions disconnect after 30 minutes of inactivity. Do not store files on lab machines; they are reimaged every Sunday.
