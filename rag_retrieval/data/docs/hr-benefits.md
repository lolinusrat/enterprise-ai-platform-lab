Halcyon pays 90% of medical, dental and vision premiums for employees and 75% for dependants. The 401(k) plan matches 100% of contributions up to 4% of salary, vesting immediately.

Other benefits include a $1,000 annual learning budget, a $50 monthly wellbeing allowance and life insurance at twice base salary.
