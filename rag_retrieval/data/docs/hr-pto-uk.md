Employees on UK contracts receive 25 days of annual leave plus the eight UK bank holidays. Leave is requested in Workday and the leave year runs from January to December.

Up to 5 days can be carried into the next leave year with manager approval. Statutory holiday rules apply where they are more generous than this policy.
