---
title: Sales discount approval matrix
department: Sales
classification: confidential
groups: sales, finance, leadership
updated: 2026-02-01
---
Account executives may offer up to 15% off list price on new contracts. Discounts from 15% to 30% need sales director approval. Anything above 30%, or multi-year price locks, needs approval from the CRO and Finance.

Non-standard payment terms longer than net 60 are treated as an additional discount and follow the same matrix.
