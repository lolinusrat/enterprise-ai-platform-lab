title: Bring your own device policy
department: IT
classification: internal
groups: all-employees
updated: 2025-09-14
---
You may use a personal phone or tablet for email, calendar and Slack once it is enrolled in Intune mobile device management. Enrollment creates a separate work profile that IT can wipe without touching your personal data.

Personal laptops and desktops may not be used to access source code, customer data or the production environment. Webmail access from a personal computer is allowed for reading only.
