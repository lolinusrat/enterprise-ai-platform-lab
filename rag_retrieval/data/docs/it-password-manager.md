Every employee gets a 1Password Business account. Install the desktop app and browser extension from Self Service and sign in with SSO.

Store all work passwords in your private vault. Teams can share credentials for shared accounts through a team vault managed by the team lead. Service credentials used by applications belong in Vault, not in 1Password.
