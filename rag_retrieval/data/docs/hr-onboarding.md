title: New hire onboarding checklist
department: HR
classification: internal

In your first week: collect your laptop and badge, complete security awareness training, set up Okta Verify, enrol in benefits in Workday within 30 days, and meet your onboarding buddy.

In your first month: complete the code of conduct course, read your team's handbook and agree 30-60-90 day goals with your manager.
