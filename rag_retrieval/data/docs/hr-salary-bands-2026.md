Base salary ranges for engineering levels in the US tier-1 location zone, effective 1 March 2026. L3 software engineer: $128,000 to $156,000. L4 software engineer: $152,000 to $190,000. L5 senior engineer: $185,000 to $232,000. L6 staff engineer: $225,000 to $285,000.

Other location zones apply a multiplier of 0.85 (tier 2) or 0.75 (tier 3). Offers above the band maximum need VP and HR approval.
