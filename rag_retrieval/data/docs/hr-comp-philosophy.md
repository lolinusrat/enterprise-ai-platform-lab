department: HR

Halcyon targets the 65th percentile of the market for base salary at each level, using benchmark data refreshed every year. Each level has a salary band; your manager can tell you where you sit within your band but individual bands are not published.

Salary reviews happen once a year in March, based on performance and position in band. Promotions move you into the band for the new level.
