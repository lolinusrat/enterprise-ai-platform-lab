Policy POL-IT-004 sets the rules for passwords on Halcyon accounts. Passwords must be at least 14 characters. We recommend passphrases of four or more random words. Complexity rules (symbols, digits) are no longer required.

Passwords do not expire on a schedule. You must change your password immediately if you suspect it was exposed, if it appears in a breach notification, or if IT asks you to.

Use the company 1Password account to store work credentials. Never reuse your Halcyon password on another site, and never share it with colleagues, including IT staff; IT will never ask for your password.
