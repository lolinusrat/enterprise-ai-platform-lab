Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel or buy on behalf of their team. Use the card for business expenses only, never for personal purchases, even if you plan to repay them.

Attach receipts to card transactions in the expense tool within 30 days. Cards with unreconciled transactions older than 60 days are suspended.
