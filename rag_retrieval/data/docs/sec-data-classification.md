Policy POL-SEC-002 defines four classification levels. Public: approved for anyone, such as marketing pages and published documentation. Internal: for all employees, such as most policies and runbooks. Confidential: limited to teams with a need to know, such as security plans and commercial terms. Restricted: highly sensitive, such as salary data, unannounced financial results and legal matters.

Every document must carry a classification label. Access to confidential and restricted documents is granted by group membership and clearance level.
