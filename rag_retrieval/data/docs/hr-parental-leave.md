---
title: Parental leave
department: HR
classification: internal
groups: all-employees
updated: 2025-07-01
---
All parents receive 18 weeks of fully paid leave after the birth, adoption or foster placement of a child, regardless of gender. Leave can be taken in up to three blocks within the first 12 months.

Your role is protected while you are on leave and you return to the same or an equivalent position. Tell your manager and HR at least eight weeks before the expected start date so cover can be arranged.
