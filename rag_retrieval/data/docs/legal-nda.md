Use the standard mutual NDA template from the Legal page for discussions with prospective partners and vendors. Sales may send it without Legal review if no changes are made. Send it for signature through DocuSign.

One-way NDAs, any edits to the template and NDAs in a language other than English must be reviewed by Legal.
