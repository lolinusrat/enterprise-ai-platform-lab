title: Sick leave
department: HR
classification: internal
groups: all-employees
updated: 2025-10-01
---
Tell your manager as early as possible on the first day you are unable to work, and record the absence in Workday. A doctor's note is needed for absences longer than five working days.

Employees receive full pay for up to 20 days of sick leave per year. Longer illness is covered by the short-term disability plan.
