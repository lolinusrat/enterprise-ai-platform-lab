title: Security incident response plan
department: Security
classification: confidential
groups: security, leadership
updated: 2026-03-22
---
A security incident is any event that threatens the confidentiality, integrity or availability of Halcyon or customer data. The on-call security engineer becomes incident commander and opens a private incident channel.

Notify the CISO within one hour of confirming a SEV1 security incident. Involve Legal as soon as personal data may be affected. Under GDPR the lead supervisory authority must be notified within 72 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach; Legal decides on notification and drafts customer communications.

Preserve evidence before remediation: snapshot affected hosts, export logs and do not reimage machines until forensics has signed off.
