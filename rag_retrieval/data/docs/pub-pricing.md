Starter costs $29 per user per month and includes 3 projects and community support. Team costs $99 per user per month and adds unlimited projects, SSO and business-hours support.

Enterprise (SKU HC-ENT-01) is priced per contract and adds audit logs, a dedicated support manager and the enterprise SLA. Annual billing saves 15% on Starter and Team.
