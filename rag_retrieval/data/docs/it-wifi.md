In the office, connect company laptops to the Halcyon-Corp network; it uses your device certificate, so no password is needed. Personal devices use Halcyon-Personal after Intune enrollment.

Visitors use Halcyon-Guest. Guest passwords change every Monday and are shown on the reception screens. The guest network has no access to internal systems.
