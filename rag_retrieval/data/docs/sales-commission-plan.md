---
title: FY2026 sales commission plan
department: Sales
classification: confidential
groups: sales, finance
updated: 2026-01-10
---
Account executives earn 10% of first-year annual contract value on closed-won deals. Above 100% of quota, an accelerator of 1.5x applies to every additional dollar; above 150% the accelerator rises to 2x.

Commissions are paid monthly in arrears. Deals cancelled within 90 days are clawed back.
