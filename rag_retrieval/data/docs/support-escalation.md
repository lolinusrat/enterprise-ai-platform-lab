title: Customer support escalation process
department: Support
classification: internal
groups: all-employees
updated: 2026-04-22
---
Priority 1 issues (production down for a customer) need a first response within 30 minutes, at any hour. Priority 2 issues need a response within 4 business hours.

When support cannot resolve an issue, escalate it to engineering by opening a ticket in the ESC Jira project with the customer ID, the impact and steps to reproduce. Account executives can follow the ticket but should not page engineers directly.
