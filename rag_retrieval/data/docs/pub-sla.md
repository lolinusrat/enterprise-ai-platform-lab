---
title: Halcyon Cloud service level agreement
department: Sales
classification: public
groups: everyone
updated: 2026-07-01
---
Halcyon commits to 99.95% monthly uptime for Enterprise customers and 99.9% for Team customers.

If uptime falls below the commitment, customers receive service credits: 10% of the monthly fee below the commitment, 25% below 99.0% and 50% below 95.0%. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month.
