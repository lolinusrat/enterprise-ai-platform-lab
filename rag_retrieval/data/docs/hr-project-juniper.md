Project Juniper is the confidential reorganisation plan for Q1 2027. It reduces headcount by about 8% through layoffs, mainly in Sales Operations and Support, and merges the Platform and Infrastructure teams.

Affected employees will be notified on 20 January 2027. Severance is 12 weeks of base salary plus 2 weeks per year of service. Do not discuss outside the steering group.
