Contractors receive a badge with access to their assigned office only and a Halcyon account limited to the systems their statement of work needs. Contractors do not have access to the production environment or customer data unless their contract explicitly requires it.

Submit timesheets in Fieldglass by Monday at 10:00 for the previous week; late timesheets delay payment by one cycle.

Contractors do not accrue Halcyon paid time off or company benefits. Arrange time off with your agency and tell your Halcyon manager in advance.
