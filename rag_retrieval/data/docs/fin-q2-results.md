Halcyon reported Q2 FY2026 revenue of $389 million, up 16% year over year. Gross margin was 73.5% and operating loss was $14 million.

Guidance for Q3 FY2026 is revenue of $398 to $404 million. The full earnings release and recording of the call are on the investor relations site.
