Declare an incident in Slack with /incident when customers are affected or an SLO is burning fast. The person who declares it is incident commander until they hand over.

Severity: SEV1 is a full outage or data loss, SEV2 is major degradation for many customers, SEV3 is minor impact. For SEV1 and SEV2 the commander assigns a communications lead who updates the public status page every 30 minutes. Every SEV1 and SEV2 gets a blameless postmortem within five working days.
