---
title: How to report phishing
department: Security
classification: internal
groups: all-employees
updated: 2026-01-05
---
If you receive a suspicious email, for example one asking you to sign in, open an unexpected invoice or buy gift cards, do not click links or open attachments. Use the "Report Phish" button in Outlook, or forward the email as an attachment to phish@halcyon.example.

If you already entered your credentials, change your password straight away and tell the Security team in #security-help. Reporting quickly matters more than being sure.
