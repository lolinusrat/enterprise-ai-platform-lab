Each service team runs a weekly on-call rotation in PagerDuty with a primary and a secondary engineer. Rotations hand over on Monday at 10:00 local time.

Acknowledgement targets: SEV1 pages within 5 minutes, SEV2 within 15 minutes. If the primary does not acknowledge within 10 minutes, PagerDuty escalates to the secondary and then to the engineering manager.

Engineers receive an on-call stipend for each week on the rotation and time off in lieu after a night with more than two hours of incident work.
