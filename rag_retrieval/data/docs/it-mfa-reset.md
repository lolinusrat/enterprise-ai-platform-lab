---
title: Lost or replaced phone - resetting multi-factor authentication
department: IT
classification: internal
groups: all-employees
updated: 2026-04-18
---
If your phone is lost, stolen or replaced you will not be able to approve sign-in prompts and you will be locked out of SSO. Contact the IT ServiceDesk by phone on extension 4357, or ask a colleague to open a ticket for you.

The ServiceDesk verifies your identity on a video call with your badge or government ID visible. After verification they issue a temporary bypass code that is valid for 24 hours. Use it to sign in and register Okta Verify on your new device.

If the phone was stolen, also report it to Security so they can revoke its sessions and wipe the work profile.
