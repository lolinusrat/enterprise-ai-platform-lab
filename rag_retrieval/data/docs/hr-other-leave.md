Bereavement leave: up to 5 paid days for the death of a close family member. Jury duty: paid at full salary for the duration of service.

Sabbatical: after five years of service you may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid leave, agreed with your manager at least three months in advance. Volunteering: 2 paid days per year.
