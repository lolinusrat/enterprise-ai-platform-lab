Under GDPR and similar laws, customers and end users can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about them, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted. These are data subject access requests (DSARs).

Forward any such request to privacy@halcyon.example on the day you receive it, without replying with data yourself. The Privacy team verifies the requester's identity and must respond within 30 days.
