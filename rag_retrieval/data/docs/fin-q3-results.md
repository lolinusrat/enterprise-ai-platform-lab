Material non-public information. Q3 FY2026 revenue was $412 million, up 18% year over year, ahead of guidance of $398 to $404 million. Gross margin was 74.2%. Operating loss narrowed to $9 million.

Results will be announced on 29 October 2026. Trading in company stock is prohibited for all insiders until two trading days after the announcement.
