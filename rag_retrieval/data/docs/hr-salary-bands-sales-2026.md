On-target earnings for sales roles in the US tier-1 location zone, effective 1 March 2026. Account executive: $150,000 to $190,000 OTE with a 50/50 base and variable split. Senior account executive: $190,000 to $250,000 OTE.

Sales development representatives: $85,000 to $105,000 OTE with a 70/30 split.
