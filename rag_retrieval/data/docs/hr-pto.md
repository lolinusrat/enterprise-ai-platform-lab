title: Paid time off policy (2026)
department: HR
classification: internal

updated: 2026-01-02
---
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per calendar year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated amount.

You can carry over up to 5 unused days into the next year; they must be used by 31 March. Request time off in Workday at least two weeks ahead for absences longer than three days. Sick leave is separate from vacation and does not reduce your balance.
