title: Open source license policy
department: Legal
classification: internal
groups: engineering
updated: 2026-01-12
---
Permissive licenses such as MIT, BSD and Apache-2.0 may be used in any product without review. Libraries under weak copyleft licenses such as LGPL and MPL need review when they are modified or statically linked.

Strong copyleft licenses are restricted: GPL code needs Legal review before it ships, and AGPL code must not be used in any product or service we offer to customers. The dependency scanner blocks builds that add an AGPL package.
