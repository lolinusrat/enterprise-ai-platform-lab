---
title: FY2027 budget planning process
department: Finance
classification: confidential
groups: finance, leadership
updated: 2026-09-01
---
Department heads submit FY2027 operating budgets and headcount requests by 14 November 2026. Finance consolidates the plan and presents it to the executive team on 5 December.

Assume 4% merit increases and 3% vendor inflation. New headcount requests must include a business case and the quarter in which the hire is needed.
