Brightwater Analytics has filed a breach of contract claim against Halcyon. All recipients of this notice must preserve every document, email, chat message and file related to the Brightwater account from January 2024 onwards.

Automatic deletion has been suspended for the listed mailboxes and Slack channels. Do not delete, edit or move relevant material, and contact the Legal team before discussing the case with anyone.
