---
title: Employee relations investigation procedure
department: HR
classification: confidential
groups: hr
updated: 2025-11-20
---
When a complaint of misconduct is received, HR assigns an investigator who is not in the reporting line of either party. Interviews are documented and stored in the restricted case system.

The investigation should conclude within 30 days. Outcomes are shared with the complainant and the respondent separately, at a level of detail appropriate to each.
