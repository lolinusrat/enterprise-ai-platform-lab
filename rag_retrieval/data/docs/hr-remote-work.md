title: Remote and hybrid work policy
department: HR
classification: internal
groups: all-employees
updated: 2026-04-01
---
Hybrid employees work from the office on two anchor days each week, chosen by their team. Fully remote roles are agreed at hiring.

You may work from another country for up to 30 days per year, with approval from your manager and HR. Longer stays create tax and immigration obligations and are usually not possible. Request approval through the Work Abroad form in Workday at least three weeks in advance.
