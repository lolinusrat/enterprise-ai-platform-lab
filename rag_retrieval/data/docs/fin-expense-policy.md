title: Travel and expense policy (POL-FIN-011)
department: Finance
classification: internal
groups: all-employees
updated: 2026-01-15
---
Book business travel through Navan. Economy class is the standard for flights under 6 hours; business class for longer flights needs VP approval.

Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to $75 per day in the US and the local equivalent elsewhere. Alcohol is not reimbursed except at approved client events.

Submit expense reports with itemised receipts within 30 days of the expense. Reports submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed.
